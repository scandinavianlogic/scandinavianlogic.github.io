Logic and AI (LAI) research group of the University of Bergen is looking for a
PhD candidate within the general area of ‘Logics for Multi-Agent Systems’ that
includes, but not limited to, various flavours of modal logics (temporal logics,
logics for strategic reasoning, epistemic logics, etc.) and their application to
verification, synthesis, mechanism design, legal reasoning, and so on. The
position is fully funded for 4 years with 25% of time is reserved for teaching.
The expected start of the position is in the beginning of 2027.
