The submission site is now open for the CLMPST meeting that will be held in
Kobe, Japan, from August 23-28. The call for papers is available here:  
<https://clmpst2027.org/call-for-papers/>

We welcome proposals for papers and symposia from across all areas of logic,
philosophy of science, and philosophy of technology. We aim to feature
presentations from across the wide array of topics pertaining to our fields,
from mathematical logic to metaphilosophy, from the foundations of the exact
sciences to philosophy of the humanities and social sciences, from philosophy of
technology to historical perspectives on the sciences, presented by researchers
from around the world. We are particularly enthusiastic about proposals that
reach across disciplines, that incorporate speakers from different countries or
regions, that explore public engagement and/or social implications of academic
work, or that reflect on relationships and contributions of different knowledge
systems.

Proposals should be submitted by November 15, 2026.

Questions can be addressed to Kevin Elliott (<kce@msu.edu>) or Fenrong Liu
(<fenronguva@gmail.com>).
