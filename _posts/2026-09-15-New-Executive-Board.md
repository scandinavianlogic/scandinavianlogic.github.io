title: "New Executive Board of the Society"
---
Following the recent election, the Scandinavian Logic Society has a new
Executive Board for the coming term. The new board is composed as follows:

### Executive committee
- President: Nina Gierasimczuk, Technical University of Denmark, Denmark
- Vice-President: Graham Leigh, University of Gothenburg, Sweden
- Secretary: Thomas Ågotnes, University of Bergen, Norway
- Treasurer: Antonis Achilleos, Reykjavik, Iceland

### Members and National Representatives:
- Salvatore Florio, University of Oslo, Norway
- Juha Kontinen, University of Helsinki, Finland
- Lars Kristiansen, University of Oslo, Norway
- Kerkko Luosto, Tampere University, Finland
- Jørgen Villadsen, Technical University of Denmark, Denmark

### Auditors
- Auditor: Fernando Velzquez-Quesada, University of Bergen, Norway
- Substitute Auditor: Annika Kanckos, University of Helsinki, Finland

The new Executive Board will oversee the activities of the Scandinavian Logic
Society and work to support research, education, and collaboration in logic
across the Nordic countries.
